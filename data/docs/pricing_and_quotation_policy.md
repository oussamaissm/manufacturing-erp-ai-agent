# Pricing and Quotation Policy

## List prices
List prices are kept in the ERP in the Standard Selling price list. Buying prices from suppliers are kept in the Standard Buying price list.

## Discounts
- Quantity discount: 3 percent from 100 units of the same item on one order, 5 percent from 500 units.
- Framework contracts (for example Iberia Rail) have their own prices and payment terms for the contract period.
- Any other discount above 5 percent needs the sales manager's approval; above 10 percent the plant manager's approval.

## Minimum order
Minimum order value is 250 EUR. Smaller orders get a 35 EUR handling fee.

## Quotations
- A quotation is valid for 30 days.
- The quoted delivery date must take into account the supplier lead time of any component not in stock plus the manufacturing lead time.
- New parts need a drawing, the yearly quantity and the material before a price is given.

## Payment terms
Standard terms are 30 days. Key accounts may have 45 days and Tier 1 aerospace accounts 60 days. New customers outside the EU pay by prepayment or letter of credit; established accounts can get open terms with the finance manager's approval. Each customer's terms are in the customer directory.

## Price changes
Prices are reviewed every January. Customers are told at least 30 days before a price change. A raw material surcharge can be applied when steel, aluminium or titanium prices rise more than 10 percent in a quarter.
