# Personal Protective Equipment and Workplace Safety

## Minimum PPE in production areas
Safety shoes (S3) and safety glasses at all times. Hearing protection where the floor marking shows the ear symbol (saw, press and welding areas). No loose clothing, jewellery or long hair outside a cap near rotating machines.

## Task-specific PPE
- Handling sheet metal, bar ends or chips: cut-resistant gloves (SAFETY-GLOVES, EN 388 level C).
- Never wear gloves when operating a lathe or a drill: they can be caught by the rotating part.
- Welding: helmet, leather gloves, flame-retardant clothing.
- Powder coating: dust mask FFP2 and coverall in the booth.
- Handling coolant, oil or paint: nitrile gloves.
- Forklift: high-visibility vest for drivers and pedestrians in the warehouse.

## Issue and replacement
PPE is issued free of charge by the shift supervisor and logged. Damaged PPE is replaced immediately. Gloves are a consumable item in the ERP and are reordered like any other stock item.

## Visitors
Visitors wear safety glasses and a visitor vest, stay on the green walkways and are always accompanied.

## Lone working
Nobody works alone on a machine in production outside shift hours. Unattended night running is allowed only on CNC-03 and WELD-01 with the safety light barriers active.

## Reporting
Missing guards, damaged PPE and near misses are reported in the incident log before the end of the shift.
