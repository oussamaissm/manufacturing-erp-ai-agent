# Purchasing and Supplier Lead Times

This document complements the stock and reorder policy. Lead times are in working days from order to delivery at our warehouse.

## Standard lead times
- Steel bar 20mm and 12mm, aluminium plate, aluminium bar: 5 days.
- Steel sheet 3mm: 7 days.
- Stainless steel rod and brass rod: 8 days.
- Copper tube: 6 days.
- Titanium rod: 25 days. It is ordered with a certificate and is never available from a local stockist.
- Ball bearings 6204: 10 days (imported). Ball bearings 6205 and 6302: 7 days.
- Seals, O-rings, springs, gaskets: 10 days.
- Fasteners (bolts, nuts, washers): 4 days.
- Hydraulic oil drums: 3 days. Hydraulic filters: 6 days.
- Cutting tools (end mills, inserts, drills): 5 days.
- Packaging (boxes, pallets, stretch film): 3 days.
- Paint and welding wire: 6 days.

## Purchase request rules
When an item falls below its safety stock, the purchase request is raised the same working day. Orders above 5000 euros need the plant manager's approval. Orders for titanium or any aerospace-certified material need the quality manager's approval of the supplier.

## Expediting
If an open sales order needs an item whose lead time is longer than the time left before the delivery date, the buyer must tell the sales manager at once and ask the supplier about express delivery. Express freight is paid by the plant only for priority accounts.

## Supplier evaluation
Suppliers are scored quarterly on on-time delivery (target 95 percent) and quality (target under 1 percent rejected lots). A supplier below target twice in a row is placed on a watch list.
