# Welding and Powder-Coating Cell Guidelines (WELD-01, PAINT-01)

## Robotic welding cell WELD-01
- The cell is used mainly for L-brackets and welded assemblies. Welding wire (WELD-WIRE-1KG) is changed when a spool is below 10 percent.
- The light barrier and door interlock must be working. Never bypass the interlock.
- Daily checks: contact tip, nozzle spatter, gas flow (shield gas pressure 2 to 3 bar), wire feed rollers.
- Welders wear a welding helmet, leather gloves and flame-retardant clothing. Nearby staff are protected by the welding screens.
- First weld of each batch is checked visually and by a macro-etch sample once per week.

## Powder-coating booth PAINT-01
- Parts are degreased and dried before coating. Coating thickness target is 60 to 80 micrometres, checked with a thickness gauge.
- Paint RAL 5010 (PAINT-RAL5010-5L) is the standard colour for brackets and flanges. A different colour needs the sales manager's approval.
- Oven curing: 200 degrees Celsius for 15 minutes after the part reaches temperature.
- The extraction filters are checked weekly and replaced when the pressure alarm trips.

## Machine status
If PAINT-01 is in Problem or Maintenance status, flanges and brackets wait in the finished goods hold area and are not shipped unpainted unless the customer agrees in writing.
