# 5S and Housekeeping

## The five steps
1. Sort: remove what is not needed at the workstation. Red-tag unknown items for 1 week, then remove them.
2. Set in order: a marked place for every tool, fixture and gauge. Shadow boards at every machine.
3. Shine: clean the workstation at the end of every shift (see shift handover).
4. Standardise: the same layout and labels on machines of the same family.
5. Sustain: monthly 5S audit by the shift supervisors, scored out of 25.

## Audit targets
Each area must score at least 20 out of 25. An area below 15 gets an action plan within one week.

## Floor rules
Walkways stay clear. Pallets are never left on walkways or in front of fire exits, extinguishers or electrical cabinets. Chips are cleaned before they reach the walkway.
