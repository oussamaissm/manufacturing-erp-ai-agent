# Measuring Instrument Calibration

All instruments used to accept or reject parts must be calibrated and labelled with the next due date. An instrument past its due date is removed from use at once.

## Calibration intervals
- Micrometers and callipers: every 6 months.
- Dial gauges and height gauges: every 6 months.
- Thread and plug gauges: every 12 months.
- Surface plates: every 12 months.
- Coordinate measuring machine QC-01: every 12 months by an accredited laboratory, plus a weekly check with a reference sphere.
- Torque wrenches: every 12 months or after 5000 cycles.

## Daily and weekly checks
Operators verify their micrometer against a gauge block at the start of each shift. QC-01 is warmed up for 30 minutes and checked with the reference sphere every Monday. A failed check blocks measurements until the quality technician has recalibrated.

## Out-of-tolerance instruments
If an instrument is found out of tolerance at calibration, the quality manager reviews every batch it accepted since the last good calibration. Affected batches are re-inspected, and customers are informed if shipped parts are impacted.

## Records
Calibration certificates are stored for 5 years and linked to the instrument serial number in the quality records.
