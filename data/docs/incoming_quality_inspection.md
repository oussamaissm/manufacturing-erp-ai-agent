# Incoming Quality Inspection

All raw materials and purchased components are inspected before they are put into stock. Material that has not passed inspection stays in the quarantine area and must not be used in production.

## Inspection levels
- Level A (full check, every delivery): titanium rod, stainless steel rod, bearings for aerospace orders, any new supplier.
- Level B (sampling): steel bar, steel sheet, aluminium bar and plate, brass and copper, seals and O-rings, fasteners.
- Level C (visual check and certificate only): consumables such as cutting fluid, grease, gloves, cardboard and pallets.

## Sampling plan
For level B, inspect the square root of the lot size, rounded up, with a minimum of 5 pieces. Accept the lot if no critical defect is found and at most 1 major defect is found in the sample. With a major defect count of 2 or more, inspect the full lot or return it.

## What is checked
1. Quantity and part reference against the delivery note and the purchase order.
2. Material certificate (3.1 certificate required for titanium, stainless steel and any aerospace order).
3. Dimensions with calibrated instruments. Critical dimensions on the coordinate measuring machine QC-01.
4. Visual condition: corrosion, damaged packaging, wrong labels.

## Outcome
Accepted material is booked into the warehouse in the ERP the same day. A rejected lot is tagged red, moved to the quarantine shelf, and a non-conformance report is opened (see the non-conformance and returns procedure). Purchasing is told within 24 hours.
