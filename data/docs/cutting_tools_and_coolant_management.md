# Cutting Tools and Coolant Management

## Cutting tools
- Carbide inserts CNMG (CUT-INSERT-CNMG) are used on the lathes for steel and stainless steel. Change an insert when flank wear reaches 0.3 mm or after the tool life set in the program, whichever comes first.
- End mills 10 mm (END-MILL-10) are used on CNC-01, CNC-02 and CNC-03. Do not regrind carbide end mills more than twice.
- HSS drill bits (DRILL-HSS-8) are used for aluminium and mild steel only. Use carbide drills for stainless steel and titanium.
- Titanium is machined at low cutting speed with plenty of coolant. Never let a tool rub without cutting, as this work-hardens the surface.

## Tool stock
Tools are issued from the tool cabinet against a signed log. Used inserts and worn end mills are collected for recycling. The tool-room clerk checks stock weekly and reports items under the safety stock level.

## Cutting fluid
- Cutting fluid (CUT-FLUID-20L) is mixed at 6 to 8 percent concentration. Check with the refractometer daily.
- Check the pH weekly: it should stay between 8.5 and 9.5. A drop below 8.5 means bacterial growth. Treat or replace the coolant.
- Skim tramp oil weekly. Replace the tank contents every 3 months or when the smell becomes unpleasant.
- Wear gloves (SAFETY-GLOVES) when handling coolant to avoid skin irritation.

## Disposal
Spent coolant is collected by the licensed waste contractor. It is never poured into the drain.
