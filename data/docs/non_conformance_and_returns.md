# Non-Conformance Reports and Customer Returns

## Non-conformance report (NCR)
Any person who finds a part, material or process that does not meet specification opens an NCR the same shift.

1. Stop using the affected material or parts and tag them red.
2. Move them to the quarantine shelf or hold area.
3. Fill in the NCR: item code, quantity, batch, machine, description of the defect, photo if possible.
4. The quality manager decides the disposition within 2 working days: use as is (with customer approval where required), rework, scrap or return to supplier.
5. Close the NCR only after the corrective action is verified.

## Scrap rate rule
If more than 2 percent of a production batch is rejected, the batch is put on hold and the quality manager reviews it before any shipment. Three NCRs on the same cause within one month trigger a root-cause analysis (5 Whys) led by the quality manager.

## Customer returns
A customer return needs a return authorisation (RMA) issued by the sales manager. Returned goods are received into quarantine, never directly into stock. The quality team inspects them within 3 working days.
- Our defect: replacement or credit note, and an NCR is opened.
- Customer error or change of mind: accepted only within 14 days, unused and in original packaging, with a 15 percent restocking fee.

## Supplier returns
Rejected lots are returned within 10 working days. Purchasing requests a replacement or credit and records the supplier claim number on the NCR.
