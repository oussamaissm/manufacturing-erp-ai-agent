# CNC Milling Centre Maintenance (CNC-01, CNC-02)

Applies to the two CNC milling centres. Maintenance is planned by the maintenance lead and logged in the ERP asset record.

## Daily checks (start of shift)
Check the coolant level and concentration. Top up if the level is below the minimum mark. Wipe the way covers and inspect the chip conveyor. Verify the spindle warm-up cycle completes without alarms before the first part is machined.

## Weekly checks
Clean the coolant tank filter and check the tool changer for wear. Lubricate the linear guides with the grease specified on the machine plate. Check the air pressure at the filter regulator: it must read between 5.5 and 6.5 bar.

## Every 500 operating hours
Replace the spindle air filter and check the drawbar clamping force. Inspect ball screw backlash and compare with the last reading. If backlash exceeds 0.03 mm, raise a maintenance request and stop 5-axis work on CNC-02.

## Spindle vibration alarm
If the spindle vibration alarm triggers, stop the programme with feed hold, never with the emergency stop unless a person is at risk. Retract the tool, check tool holder seating and tool balance, and inspect the insert for chipping. If vibration persists with a new tool, tag the machine out of service and call maintenance.
