# Shift Handover Procedure

The plant runs two shifts: morning (06:00 to 14:00) and afternoon (14:00 to 22:00). Machines that run unattended overnight are limited to CNC-03 and the welding cell WELD-01 with the safety light barriers active.

## What the outgoing supervisor reports
1. Machines stopped or in fault, and why. Update the machine status in the ERP.
2. Production progress against open work orders.
3. Quality issues, open non-conformance reports and parts on hold.
4. Safety incidents or near misses of the shift.
5. Material shortages that may block the next shift.

## Handover meeting
The two supervisors walk the shop floor together for 15 minutes. The incoming supervisor signs the handover sheet. A machine is not restarted after a fault until the incoming supervisor has understood the cause.

## Housekeeping before leaving
Every operator cleans their workstation, empties the chip bin if more than half full, wipes spilled coolant or oil, and returns tools to the tool cabinet.

## Priority rules for the next shift
Priority accounts first, then the earliest delivery date. If two orders have the same date, the order with the smaller remaining quantity goes first so that it can ship complete.
