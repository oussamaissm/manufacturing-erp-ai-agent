# Preventive Maintenance Schedule

This is the master schedule. Detailed steps are in each machine family's maintenance document. While a machine is in planned maintenance, its status in the ERP is set to Maintenance.

## Daily (operator, start of shift)
- CNC-01, CNC-02, CNC-03: coolant level and concentration, way covers, chip conveyor, spindle warm-up without alarms.
- LATHE-01, LATHE-02: chuck jaws, coolant, way-lube level.
- PRESS-01, PRESS-02: oil level in the sight glass, leaks around the cylinder and hoses.
- WELD-01: contact tip, nozzle, gas pressure 2 to 3 bar. PAINT-01: extraction filter alarm.
- QC-01: 30 minute warm-up before the first measurement.

## Weekly (operator or maintenance technician)
- CNC machines: coolant tank filter, tool changer, linear guide lubrication, air pressure 5.5 to 6.5 bar.
- Lathes: turret and tool holders, chuck and tailstock pressures, chip conveyor.
- QC-01: reference sphere check every Monday.
- PAINT-01: extraction filters.

## Monthly (maintenance technician)
- Lathes: axis backlash (limit 0.02 mm), coolant filter cartridge, belt tensioner grease.
- Presses: hydraulic filter condition indicator, hose inspection.
- Forklifts: inspection of mast chains and brakes by the maintenance team.

## By operating hours
- CNC machines every 500 hours: spindle air filter, drawbar clamping force, ball screw backlash (limit 0.03 mm).
- Presses at 1000 hours: oil sample sent for analysis.
- Presses every 2000 hours or once a year: hydraulic oil and filter change.
- Lathes every 2000 hours: spindle bearing preload check, chuck oil and coolant tank change.

## Yearly
- QC-01 calibration by an accredited laboratory.
- Electrical cabinet thermography on all machines above 10 kW.
- Fire extinguisher and emergency lighting inspection (by the HSE manager).

## Planning rule
Planned maintenance is scheduled on Friday afternoons when possible. A planned stop that would delay a Tier 1 order is moved, unless it is a safety item.
