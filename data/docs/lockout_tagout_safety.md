# Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) Safety Procedure

Mandatory for any maintenance, cleaning or repair where unexpected start-up or release of stored energy could injure a person. Applies to all machines: CNC-01, CNC-02, PRESS-01, LATHE-01 and PACK-01.

## Who can apply a lock
Only trained and authorised staff. Each person uses their own personal padlock and tag. Never use another person's lock and never remove a lock that is not yours.

## Procedure
1. Tell the operator and the shift supervisor that the machine will be shut down.
2. Stop the machine using the normal stop control.
3. Isolate all energy sources: electrical main switch, compressed air, hydraulic pressure.
4. Apply your personal padlock and tag to each isolation point.
5. Release stored energy: bleed air lines, lower the press ram onto its mechanical stops, discharge capacitors by waiting the time shown on the electrical cabinet.
6. Verify isolation by trying to start the machine. It must not start. Return the controls to off.

## Removing the lock
Check that tools and people are clear, remove your own lock and tag, then tell the operator before restarting. If the owner of a lock is absent, only the shift supervisor may authorise removal after a documented attempt to contact them.
