# Hydraulic Press Troubleshooting (PRESS-01)

The 200-tonne hydraulic press uses ISO VG 46 hydraulic oil. Oil is supplied in 20 L drums (item HYD-OIL-20L).

## Press does not reach full pressure
First check the oil level in the reservoir sight glass. Low oil is the most common cause. Then check the pressure relief valve setting against the plate value and inspect the pump suction line for air leaks. Do not adjust the relief valve above the plate value under any circumstance.

## Oil overheating
Oil temperature above 60 degrees Celsius means the cooler is not working or the oil is degraded. Check the cooler fan and clean the radiator fins. If the oil is dark or smells burnt, schedule an oil change. A full oil change needs two drums of HYD-OIL-20L, so verify stock before planning it.

## Slow or jerky ram movement
Bleed air from the cylinder following the bleed procedure on the press door label. Check the proportional valve filter. Jerky movement after bleeding points to worn seals: log a maintenance request, the press must not be used for production with leaking seals.

## Oil change interval
Change the hydraulic oil every 2000 operating hours or once a year, whichever comes first. Send a sample for analysis at 1000 hours.
