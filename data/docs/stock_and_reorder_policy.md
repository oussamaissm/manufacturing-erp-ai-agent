# Stock and Reorder Policy

## Safety stock
Each stocked item has a safety stock level in the ERP. When total stock across warehouses falls below safety stock, purchasing must raise a purchase request the same working day.

## Lead times
Steel bar and aluminium plate: 5 working days from the usual supplier. Ball bearings 6204: 10 working days because they are imported. Hydraulic oil drums: 3 working days. When an open sales order depends on an item with a longer lead time than the delivery date allows, warn the sales manager immediately.

## Order quantities
Raw materials are reordered up to twice the safety stock level. Hydraulic oil is always ordered in lots of 4 drums so that one full oil change plus a spare is covered.

## Stock counts
A cycle count of raw materials is done on the first Monday of each month. Differences above 2 percent are investigated before the ERP stock is adjusted. Only the warehouse manager can post a stock reconciliation.
