# New Employee Onboarding

## Day 1
- Welcome by the shift supervisor, site tour, emergency exits and assembly point in the car park.
- Safety induction: PPE, lockout-tagout principles, emergency stops, incident reporting.
- Issue of PPE and a locker. ERP account created by IT with the role of the job (see IT and ERP access policy).

## Week 1
- Shadow an experienced operator on the assigned machine.
- Read the maintenance document of the machine family and the shift handover procedure.
- Forklift: no driving before the forklift licence is checked.

## Training before working alone
- Machine operators: supervised production for at least 10 shifts and a sign-off by the shift supervisor.
- Quality inspectors: training on QC-01 and on the sampling plans; sign-off by the quality manager.
- Warehouse staff: forklift licence, ERP stock transactions training, chemical storage rules.

## Who to ask
- Safety and incidents: HSE manager.
- Machines and breakdowns: maintenance lead.
- Quality, non-conformance and inspection: quality manager.
- Orders and delivery dates: sales manager.
- Purchasing and suppliers: buyer.
- Production plan and priorities: production planner.
- ERP accounts and passwords: IT support.

## End of probation
After 3 months, a review with the supervisor checks the training records, safety behaviour and skills sign-offs.
