# Operations FAQ

## Why does the ERP show stock that is not on the shelf?
Usually a stock movement was not posted: a delivery shipped without its delivery note, or parts used without a material issue. Tell the warehouse manager; only they can post a stock reconciliation.

## Can I ship an order partially?
Only if the customer accepted it in writing. Tier 1 accounts can get an exception from the sales manager if at least 80 percent of a line can ship (see the order fulfilment policy).

## A machine is in Maintenance status but works. Can I use it?
No. The maintenance lead releases it and changes the status back in the ERP.

## Who can change a delivery date?
Only the sales manager, after agreeing it with the customer at least 3 working days before the original date.

## A supplier delivery is late. What do I do?
The buyer contacts the supplier the same day and tells the sales manager if an open order depends on it. Express freight is paid by the plant only for priority accounts.

## How do I know when ordered material will arrive?
The open purchase order in the ERP shows the expected date. The standard lead times are in the purchasing and supplier lead times document and in the supplier directory.

## A customer asks for a certificate. Where is it?
Material certificates (3.1) are stored by batch in the quality records. Titanium and stainless steel always have one.

## Can the AI assistant change data in the ERP?
No. It is read-only: it can look up machines, orders, stock and documents, but it cannot create or change anything.
