# Incident and Emergency Response

## Emergency stop
Every machine has red mushroom emergency-stop buttons. Press the nearest one immediately if a person is in danger. After an emergency stop, the machine is restarted only by the shift supervisor after checking that the area is clear.

## Injury
1. Stop the machine and make the area safe.
2. Call the first aider (list posted at every entrance) and, if serious, call emergency services.
3. Do not move a seriously injured person unless there is immediate danger.
4. Tell the shift supervisor. The supervisor informs the HSE manager within the hour.

## Fire
- Small fire on a machine or electrical cabinet: use the CO2 extinguisher. Never use water.
- Hydraulic oil fire: use the foam or CO2 extinguisher and cut the power.
- If the fire is not out within 30 seconds, activate the alarm, evacuate to the assembly point in the car park and call the fire brigade.

## Hydraulic oil leak
Stop the press, apply lockout-tagout, contain the oil with absorbent granules, and dispose of the soaked absorbent in the marked waste drum. Report any leak of more than 2 litres to the HSE manager.

## Incident reporting
All accidents and near misses are reported in the incident log before the end of the shift. The HSE manager reviews them weekly. Serious accidents are investigated within 5 working days and corrective actions are tracked until closed.
