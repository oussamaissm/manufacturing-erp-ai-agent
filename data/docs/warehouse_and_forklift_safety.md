# Warehouse and Forklift Safety

## Forklift operation
- Only operators with a valid forklift licence may drive. The licence is checked yearly.
- Do the daily check before use: brakes, horn, lights, forks, mast chains, tyres, hydraulic leaks. A forklift with a fault is tagged and not used.
- Seat belt on at all times. Maximum speed 6 km/h inside the building and 3 km/h in aisles with pedestrians.
- Forks low (15 cm) while travelling. Never carry a passenger. Never lift a person on the forks.
- The load must be stable and strapped, and must not exceed the capacity shown on the data plate.

## Pedestrians
Pedestrians use the marked green walkways and wear high-visibility vests in the warehouse. Make eye contact with the driver before crossing.

## Racking and storage
- Heavy items (steel bar bundles, pallets of castings) go on the lowest racking levels.
- Maximum stacking height for EUR pallets is 3 pallets on the floor, never on unmarked areas.
- Racking damage is reported at once. A damaged upright is unloaded and blocked off until inspected.
- Keep fire exits, extinguishers and electrical cabinets clear for 1 metre.

## Hydraulic oil and chemicals
Drums of hydraulic oil, cutting fluid and paint are stored in the bunded chemical area, never on general racking. Safety data sheets are kept at the chemical area entrance.

## Battery charging
Forklift batteries are charged only in the charging bay. No smoking, no naked flame. Wear gloves and eye protection when handling battery acid.
