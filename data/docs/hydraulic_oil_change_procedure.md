# Hydraulic Oil Change Procedure (PRESS-01, PRESS-02)

## When
Change the hydraulic oil every 2000 operating hours or once a year, whichever comes first, or immediately if oil analysis shows contamination. Replace the hydraulic filter (HYD-FILTER-H20) at the same time.

## Materials
One oil change on PRESS-01 (200t press) uses 2 drums of 20 L (HYD-OIL-20L). The 100t press PRESS-02 uses 1 drum. Oil is ordered in lots of 4 drums so that a full PRESS-01 change plus spares is always covered. Also needed: one HYD-FILTER-H20, absorbent granules, a clean funnel and a waste drum.

## Procedure
1. Warm the oil by running the press unloaded for 10 minutes. Warm oil drains faster and carries more contamination out.
2. Stop the press, lower the ram onto its mechanical stops and apply lockout-tagout.
3. Place the drain tray under the tank drain plug and drain the oil completely.
4. Remove and replace the hydraulic filter. Wipe the housing before refitting.
5. Clean the tank interior with a lint-free cloth. Do not use water or solvents.
6. Refit the drain plug with a new seal and refill through the filter funnel to the upper mark of the sight glass.
7. Remove the lockout, start the press and cycle the ram 10 times without load to bleed the air.
8. Check the oil level again and top up. Check for leaks at the filter and plug.
9. Record the date, hours and oil quantity in the maintenance log.

## Disposal
Used oil goes to the waste oil drum for the licensed collector. Never pour oil into drains.
