# Quality Control Plans

What is measured on each product, how and how often. Measuring instruments must be calibrated (see measuring instrument calibration). A batch with more than 2 percent rejected parts is put on hold (see non-conformance and returns).

## GEAR-SHAFT-A
- Critical: bearing seat diameter 20 mm, tolerance k6, measured with a micrometer on 1 part in 10.
- Critical: hardness 55 to 60 HRC after heat treatment, 2 parts per batch.
- Major: overall length 180 mm plus or minus 0.1 mm.
- First-article inspection for every batch before the run continues.

## VALVE-BODY-B
- Critical: bore diameter 32 mm H7 and bore position, measured on QC-01, first part and 1 in 20.
- Major: flatness of the sealing face 0.02 mm.
- First-article inspection for every batch.

## PUMP-HOUSING-P1
- Critical: bearing bore 52 mm H7 on QC-01, 1 part in 10.
- Major: leak test of the assembled housing at 6 bar for 60 seconds, every part.

## FLANGE-DN80
- Major: bolt circle diameter 160 mm and hole positions, 1 part in 25.
- Coating thickness 60 to 80 micrometres, 3 readings per batch.

## HYD-CYL-50
- Critical: pressure test at 1.5 times working pressure (240 bar for 2 minutes), every cylinder.
- Major: rod surface free of scratches, visual check on every rod.

## BRACKET-L-STD
- Major: angle 90 degrees plus or minus 0.5, 1 part in 50.
- Weld visual check on the first part of every batch, macro-etch sample once a week.

## TURBINE-HUB-T2
- Critical: full dimensional report on QC-01 for every part.
- Critical: 3.1 material certificate from the titanium supplier linked to the batch.
- Surface roughness Ra 0.8 micrometres on ground faces after GRIND-01.

## MANIFOLD-M4
- Critical: port positions on QC-01, first part and 1 in 10.
- Major: leak test at 1.5 times working pressure on every manifold.

## Records
Inspection results are recorded against the batch. Records are kept 10 years for aerospace and medical customers and 5 years for all others.
