# Order Fulfilment and Quality Policy

## Delivery commitments
The delivery date on a sales order is a commitment to the customer. When stock or capacity makes a date impossible, the sales manager must contact the customer at least 3 working days before the delivery date to agree a new date. Do not move the date in the ERP without that agreement.

## Partial deliveries
Partial deliveries are allowed only if the customer accepted them in writing. Without that acceptance, ship complete orders. If at least 80 percent of a line can be shipped and the customer is a priority account, ask the sales manager for an exception.

## Priority accounts
Hexa Aero is a priority account: its orders are scheduled first on the shared CNC machines. Other orders are scheduled by delivery date, earliest first.

## Quality control
Every batch of gear shafts (GEAR-SHAFT-A) and valve bodies (VALVE-BODY-B) needs a first-article inspection before the production run continues. A batch with more than 2 percent rejected parts is put on hold and reviewed by the quality manager before shipment.
