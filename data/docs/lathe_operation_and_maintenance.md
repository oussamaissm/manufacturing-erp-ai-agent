# CNC Lathe Operation and Maintenance (LATHE-01, LATHE-02)

Applies to LATHE-01 (single-spindle CNC lathe) and LATHE-02 (twin-spindle CNC lathe). Both are used for turning shafts, flanges and cylinder rods.

## Start of shift checks
1. Check the chuck jaws for chips and wear, and clean the jaw seats.
2. Check the coolant level and concentration (target 6 to 8 percent on the refractometer).
3. Check the way-lube level on the gauge and top up if it is below the minimum mark.
4. Run the spindle warm-up program for 10 minutes if the machine was off for more than 4 hours.

## Weekly maintenance
- Clean and inspect the tool turret and tool holders.
- Check the tailstock pressure and the chuck clamping pressure against the values on the machine plate.
- Clean the chip conveyor and empty the chip bin.

## Monthly maintenance
- Check the backlash on both axes. If it exceeds 0.02 mm, raise a maintenance request.
- Replace the coolant filter cartridge if the pressure drop warning is on.
- Grease the spindle belt tensioner with lithium grease (GREASE-LITHIUM-1KG).

## Every 2000 operating hours
The maintenance team checks the spindle bearing preload and replaces the hydraulic chuck oil and the coolant tank contents. Record the work in the maintenance log and update the ERP machine status to Maintenance for the duration.

## Common faults
- Vibration marks on the part: check insert wear first, then chuck clamping pressure, then reduce feed by 10 percent.
- Dimensional drift during the shift: wait for thermal stabilisation, measure the first part after 30 minutes, then apply a tool offset.
- Chuck pressure alarm: stop the machine, apply lockout-tagout, then check the hydraulic unit level.
