# Shipping and Packaging Procedure

## Before packing
1. Confirm in the ERP that the sales order is complete and that stock covers every line. Partial shipments need the customer's written acceptance.
2. Check that the final inspection record exists for the batch. Gear shafts and valve bodies need the first-article inspection record.
3. Print the delivery note and the packing labels.

## Packing rules
- Small parts (brackets, flanges): large cardboard boxes (CARDBOARD-BOX-L), maximum 20 kg per box.
- Heavy parts (hydraulic cylinders, pump housings, turbine hubs): wooden-base crate or EUR pallet (PALLET-EUR) with strapping.
- Pallets are wrapped with at least 3 turns of stretch film (STRETCH-FILM) and a maximum height of 1.6 metres.
- Machined surfaces are coated with rust-preventive oil and wrapped in VCI paper for export shipments.
- Aerospace orders (Hexa Aero, Toulouse Aerostructures): each part is individually bagged and labelled with the batch number and certificate reference.

## Palletising
Palletising is done on PACK-02 (palletising robot). When PACK-02 is in maintenance, pallets are built manually by two people using the lifting aid.

## Dispatch
The carrier pickup time is booked 24 hours ahead. The delivery note is signed by the driver, and a copy is scanned into the ERP. The shipping clerk marks the order as delivered the same day.

## Damage and shortage claims
The customer must report visible damage or shortages within 48 hours. Photos of the packaging and the goods are required to open a claim.
