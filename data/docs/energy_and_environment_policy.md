# Energy and Environment Policy

## Energy
- The largest consumers are the powder-coating oven PAINT-01 (40 kW), PRESS-01 (30 kW) and CNC-02 (22 kW).
- Machines left idle more than 30 minutes are switched to standby. The oven of PAINT-01 is not heated for fewer than 20 parts unless the order is urgent.
- Compressed air leaks are reported and fixed within a week: a 3 mm leak costs about 1500 EUR a year.

## Waste streams
- Metal chips and offcuts: sorted by material (steel, stainless, aluminium, brass, titanium) in marked bins and sold to the scrap dealer. Never mix titanium with other chips.
- Used hydraulic oil and spent coolant: collected by a licensed contractor. Never poured into drains.
- Oily absorbents and filters: hazardous waste drum.
- Cardboard and stretch film: recycling bins at the shipping area.

## Chemicals
Oil, coolant and paint are stored in the bunded chemical area with their safety data sheets. Spills above 2 litres are reported to the HSE manager.

## Targets
Reduce electricity per machine hour by 5 percent a year and keep the scrap rate below 2 percent. Results are reviewed quarterly by the plant manager.
