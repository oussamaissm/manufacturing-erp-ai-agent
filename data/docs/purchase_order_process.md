# Purchase Order Process

## From shortage to purchase order
1. When an item falls below its safety stock, the buyer raises a purchase request the same working day.
2. The quantity brings stock up to twice the safety stock, rounded up to a multiple of the supplier's minimum order quantity. Hydraulic oil is ordered in lots of 4 drums.
3. The buyer creates the purchase order in the ERP for the supplier listed on the item, with the expected date from the supplier's lead time.
4. Approvals: above 5000 EUR the plant manager approves; titanium or aerospace-certified material needs the quality manager's approval of the supplier.
5. The order is sent to the supplier, who confirms the date. The confirmation number is recorded on the purchase order.

## Receiving
Goods are checked at receiving (incoming quality inspection) and booked with a purchase receipt against the purchase order. Rejected goods go to quarantine and are never booked into stock.

## Invoices
The supplier invoice is paid only when it matches the purchase order and the receipt (three-way match). Price differences above 2 percent are checked with the buyer.

## Following up open orders
Open purchase orders are reviewed every Monday. Orders past their expected date are chased the same day. If a late delivery puts a sales order at risk, the buyer tells the sales manager at once.
