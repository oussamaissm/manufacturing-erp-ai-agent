# Customer Priority and Service Levels

## Priority tiers
- Tier 1 (priority accounts): Hexa Aero and Toulouse Aerostructures. Their orders are scheduled first on shared CNC machines, and express freight may be paid by the plant if a date is at risk.
- Tier 2 (key accounts): Atlas Mecanique, Nordic Pumps, Helios Energy, Baltic Marine, Nordland Shipyards.
- Tier 3 (standard accounts): all other commercial customers.
- Public customers such as Municipalite de Calais follow tendered delivery dates and penalties. Check the contract before moving a date.

## Service levels
- On-time delivery target: 95 percent of order lines.
- Order acknowledgement: within 1 working day.
- Customer complaint first answer: within 2 working days.
- Delivery-date change: the sales manager contacts the customer at least 3 working days before the original date.

## Allocation of scarce stock
When stock is not enough for all open orders, allocate in this order: Tier 1, then the earliest delivery date, then the smaller remaining quantity. Document every allocation decision in the sales order comments.

## Escalation
If an order will be more than 5 working days late, the sales manager informs the plant manager the same day. A late order for a Tier 1 customer is escalated immediately.
